HRMSPro - Account Deletion

Users of HRMSPro can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Contact the HRMSPro administrator using the official support channel.
  2. Provide your registered employee ID or registered email address.
  3. Clearly request permanent account and associated data deletion.
  4. The administrator will verify your request and explain the next steps.

Data Deletion

Upon verification, account information and associated personal data will be deleted, except information that must be retained for legal or regulatory reasons.

Contact

Please contact your HRMSPro administrator through your organization's official support channel.
